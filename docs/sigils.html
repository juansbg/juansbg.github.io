Omertà Sigils

Nineteen roles, one colour. Each sigil is drawn on a 24-unit grid with straight lines only: horizontals, verticals and 45° diagonals, square caps, mitred joins, a 1.5-unit stroke. No curves, because nothing else in the language has one. The ink is Neon on any dark ground and Midnight on paper. The side is still said by the fill and the glow, never by the sigil.

This is a sheet to judge against, not a finished set. Every card shows the mark at hero size, then at the five sizes and grounds it would actually live on in the app.

Straight lines

A 24-unit grid. Right angles and 45° only. Square line caps, mitre joins. Stroke 1.5.

Neon on dark

Seats, the night card, hollow marks. 15.3:1 on Midnight, so it reads at 20px.

Midnight on paper

The held role card and the report. Neon on Ledger is 1.2:1 and vanishes.

Never inside a fill

A filled crew or town mark keeps its own ink. Neon on Vendetta is 3:1.

On a seat

72px · 56px · 44px tiles, the sizes a phone actually gets

The sigil sits above the name and takes the role label's place when the tile is small. On a selected seat it inverts to Midnight with everything else. On a dead seat it dims with the tile.

What to look at
  1. Does each one read at the 24px size without the name beside it? That is the test the seat has to pass.
  2. Which ones are weakest as drawings? First candidates: the Renegade's dagger, the Orphan's umbrella, the Witness's keyhole.
  3. Is Neon the right weight next to Ledger names on a tile, or does it want the 45% dim everywhere but the night card?
  4. Should the two occult roles carry their sigil hollow-in-a-square, as their marks do, or bare like everyone else?
